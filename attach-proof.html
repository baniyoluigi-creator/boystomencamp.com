We check your payment against the bank record, which usually takes a few minutes. Once it is confirmed, your official receipt is sent to the email address you gave. Our team will also match it to your son's registration and confirm his place. If you have paid the booking fee or more, you will receive all the camp documents needed to help you prepare, at the email address you registered with. Please check your spam folder too. If you have more than one receipt, for example a balance paid later, you are welcome to upload each one here.

Withdrawals and refundsThe balance of the fee is completed 7 days before the camp. The booking fee is not refunded at any time after it is paid. Moving your son to a future camp at least 7 days before the camp costs only the booking fee. Withdrawing him more than 2 weeks before the camp deducts only the booking fee. Withdrawing less than 2 weeks and up to 2 days before the camp deducts a total of UGX 150,000, booking fee included, or USD 90 for the Summer Camp. From the day before the camp there is no refund. A boy sent home from the camp because of indiscipline is not refunded. If you withdraw your son, you may replace him with another boy who fits the camp's requirements.
