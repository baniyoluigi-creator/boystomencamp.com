How to pay for the camp

A booking fee secures his place. Follow the simple steps below, then send us your proof of payment. Where the bank or phone asks for a reference, please write his full name.

Registered already?Pay below, then send us the transaction ID with his name.
Paid already, but not yet registered?That is fine. Register him now and choose Yes under the booking fee, so we can match your payment.
Not registered and not paid?Register first. Payment details appear as soon as you submit.

Payment is by bank transfer or deposit. Not sure how to do it? Choose the way that suits you below and follow the simple steps. It takes only a few minutes.

Booking fee to secure his placeUGX 50,000 for Kampala and Arua, or USD 50 for the Summer Camp. It counts toward the total fee and is non-refundable.
Centenary Bank, Wakiso Branch
Account namePotential Unexplored Limited
Account number3100125782
  1. Open your bank app or visit your bank, or any Centenary Bank branch or agent.
  2. Choose to send money to another bank account, or to make a deposit.
  3. Enter the Centenary Bank account name and number shown above.
  4. Enter the amount for the camp and confirm.
  5. Keep your receipt or confirmation message. You will need it below.
  1. On your MTN line, dial *165#
  2. Choose Financial Services.
  3. Choose Bank Transfer and select Centenary Bank.
  4. Enter the account number shown above, then enter the amount.
  5. Enter your MoMo PIN to confirm.
  6. Wait for the MTN confirmation message and keep it. You will need it below.
  1. On your Airtel line, dial *185#
  2. Choose Financial Services, then Banks.
  3. Select Centenary Bank. If you do not see it, choose Next until it appears.
  4. Enter the account number shown above, then enter the amount.
  5. Enter your Airtel Money PIN to confirm.
  6. Wait for the Airtel confirmation message and keep it. You will need it below.
Good to know. The menu on your phone may look a little different, so follow what appears on your screen. Mobile money has daily limits and small charges. Check your limit before you start. If the camp fee is more than your limit, pay in parts on different days and send proof for each part.

After you pay

Send a screenshot of your confirmation message, or the transaction ID, to the Manager, Eduardo Fortunato, on WhatsApp or by email. Please include the name of the boy and the camp he is joining. You will receive a reply once your payment is confirmed.

  • Check the account name before you confirm any payment.
  • Keep your transaction ID safe.
  • Send your proof of payment on WhatsApp or by email.
Send proof on WhatsApp Send proof by email

The WhatsApp number and the email address are for proof of payment only. Please do not send money to them. Pay only into the Centenary Bank account shown above.
