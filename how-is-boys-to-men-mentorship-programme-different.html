A Question We Hear Often

How is the Boys to Men Mentorship Programme different?

Many programmes claim to build confidence or character. The difference is in the details, and there are a few that matter most.

Many programmes claim to build confidence or character. What actually makes the difference is usually in the details, and there are several that matter here.

We do not treat every boy the same way. A shy boy and a loud boy need different kinds of attention, and our mentors are trained to notice which is which.

We do not rely on lectures. Boys learn mostly through real experience, challenges, responsibility, and honest conversation, not sitting and being talked at.

“It is this combination, personal attention, real experience, family involvement, and follow up, that most programmes only offer pieces of.”

We stay connected with families, not just the boy, because real change needs support at home too, not just for one week away.

And we follow boys beyond camp itself, checking in, tracking their growth, because one week is a beginning, not the whole story.

It is this combination, personal attention, real experience, family involvement, and follow up, that most programmes only offer pieces of. We built ours to include all of it.
