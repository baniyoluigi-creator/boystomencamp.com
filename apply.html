Why a Summer Camp? Every intake is open to boys and young men from anywhere, including those living abroad. The Summer Camp exists because many families in the diaspora cannot fit the Ugandan school holiday dates, and their summer holiday is longer, so July gives them more time. It suits boys on their summer holidays, including those in international schools and children raised abroad who want to reconnect with the motherland. It is also a good fit for Senior 6 vacationists, preparing them to begin university life with more focus. Boys living in Uganda are welcome too.

Boys to Men Mentorship Camp serves boys and young men from 8 to 23. Kampala, Arua, and Gulu each run one intake a year at different times, with a Summer Camp every July. There is nothing for a parent to choose within a region: once boys arrive, they are placed automatically into the cluster that fits their age.

A calm, coaching-based approach

Boys to Men is not a disciplinary camp. Read our full Safeguarding commitment.

Living abroad?

You can join any intake. Kampala, Arua and Gulu have all gathered participants from beyond Uganda. If the Ugandan school holiday dates do not suit your family, the July Summer Camp gives a longer window. If you are travelling, we will guide you on travel and time zones.

Boys to Men Mentorship Camp is not a separate organisation. It is an initiative of Potential Unexplored Ltd, born out of its Life Readiness Programme for boys, girls, and adults.
