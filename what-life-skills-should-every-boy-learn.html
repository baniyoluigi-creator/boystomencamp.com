A Question We Hear Often

What life skills should every boy learn?

It is a longer list than most people expect, covering money, communication, conflict, self care, and sitting with discomfort. Here it is in full.

This question comes up often, and the honest answer is, it is a longer list than most people expect, covering several different areas of life.

He should learn how to manage money, even in a small way, so he understands saving, spending, and the value of what he has.

He should learn how to communicate clearly, saying what he means without shouting or shutting down.

“He should learn how to sit with discomfort, disappointment, boredom, failure, without needing a screen or distraction to escape it immediately.”

He should learn how to handle conflict, disagreeing with someone without losing the relationship entirely.

He should learn basic self care, cooking, cleaning, organizing his own space, so he is not helpless when nobody is there to do it for him.

And he should learn how to sit with discomfort, disappointment, boredom, failure, without needing a screen or distraction to escape it immediately.

No single lesson covers all of this. It takes repeated, real practice across many small moments, which is exactly what a structured programme, done over several days, can offer that a single conversation at home cannot.

Boys to Men Mentorship Camp is built to cover this full range, not just one or two of these skills.
