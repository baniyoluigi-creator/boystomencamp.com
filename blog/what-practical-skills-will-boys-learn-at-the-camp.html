What practical skills will boys learn at the camp?

Boys at Boys to Men take part in practical sessions such as making cakes, soap, chapati, cookies and art. Here is why practical skills matter.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Besides talks and discussions, boys at Boys to Men take part in practical sessions where they make things with their own hands. Some of these may include cakes, soap, chapati, cookies and art, among others.

We include these for two reasons. First, a boy who has made something himself learns that he can produce value and not only consume it. That is the start of an enterprising mind. Second, many boys have never cooked or cleaned, and we want them to leave more capable and more considerate at home.

Practical sessions also teach teamwork. When twelve boys must make soap together, they learn to plan, share tasks, listen and solve problems, all in an afternoon.

We do not promise a fixed list of skills, because sessions can change from one camp to another. What we do promise is that your son will use his hands, work with others, and see that skills can become income.

If you have a particular interest for your son, tell us. We will see what is possible.
