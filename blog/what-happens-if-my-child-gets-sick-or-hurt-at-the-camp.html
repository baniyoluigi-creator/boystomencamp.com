A Question Parents Ask Us

What happens if my child gets sick or hurt at the camp?

What a parent should ask about illness and injury at the camp, and how Boys to Men handles medical information before the camp begins.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

This is one of the first things a parent should ask, and the answer should be clear before you pay.

Every boy attending Boys to Men brings a Basic Medical Examination Form completed by a clinician before the camp begins. It tells us about his health, any condition and any medicine he needs. Please be fully honest on the form, because it exists to protect your son.

Also tell us about anything not on the form, such as an allergy, a fear, or a recent illness. Give us a number where you can be reached during the week.

Whichever camp you choose, ask these things. Who looks after a child who feels unwell? How quickly can a child reach a clinic? How will you be told? Does the camp keep medicines safely and give them correctly? A director who has thought about these questions will answer without hesitation.

If you have a specific health worry about your son, call us before you register so that we can talk it through together.
