How do I prepare my son for the camp?

Simple steps parents can take to prepare a boy for a residential camp, from talking about the week to packing and keeping in touch.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Good preparation turns nerves into excitement. Here is what works.

Talk about the camp early. Show him the website and the photos and tell him honestly what the week is about. Let him ask questions, and write down the ones you cannot answer so you can ask us.

Once your family has paid the booking fee, we send the list of requirements so that you know what to pack. Let him help pack. A boy who knows where his things are feels more in control.

Complete the medical form with your clinician in good time. Do not leave it for the last night.

Agree on how you will stay in touch and what you will do on closing day. Tell him you are proud that he is going.

Finally, check that your own feelings are in order. Children read their parents' worry very quickly. If you are calm and positive, he is more likely to be as well.
