A Question We Hear Often

Why should I send my son to a mentorship camp?

Whichever reason brought you here, camp usually ends up addressing more than one of them at once. Here is why.

Parents weighing this decision usually have more than one reason pulling them toward it, even if they only mention one out loud.

Some want their son to have a male role model outside the home, someone who can reach him in a way they currently cannot.

Some are worried about the friends and influences already shaping him, and want to offer something stronger to counter that.

“Build his confidence, and his friendships often improve too. Build his sense of purpose, and his responsibility often follows.”

Some simply sense that their son is carrying something he has not been able to say out loud, and want him somewhere safe to finally say it.

And some are thinking ahead, wanting their son to walk into adulthood already steady, rather than hoping he figures it out on his own later.

Whichever reason brought you here, camp usually ends up addressing more than one of them at once, because a boy's growth rarely happens in just one area. Build his confidence, and his friendships often improve too. Build his sense of purpose, and his responsibility often follows.

Boys to Men Mentorship Camp was built to work on the whole boy, not just one problem at a time.
