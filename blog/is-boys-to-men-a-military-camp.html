A Question Parents Ask Us

Is Boys to Men a military camp?

Boys to Men is not a military or disciplinary camp, yet it holds high standards of discipline. Here is what a day looks like for the boys.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

No. Boys to Men is neither a military camp nor a disciplinary camp. Nobody is shouted at, and boys are not sent to us as a punishment. We do, however, hold the highest standards of discipline, because discipline is part of becoming a man.

The difference is in how we teach it. Boys keep the place where they stay clean. They wash their own plates. They help with breakfast and dinner and wash utensils, while our staff prepare lunch. They help to receive their parents on closing day. They are expected to apologise when they are wrong and to say thank you when they are helped.

These small habits matter. A boy who learns to clean up after himself, apologise and show gratitude at the camp is far more likely to do so at home.

If you are looking for a strict military style programme, we are not that. If you want your son to grow in character with firm and respectful guidance, we may be what you are looking for.
