How much does a boys mentorship camp cost in Uganda?

Fees for the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp in Uganda for December 2026, January 2027 and July 2027, how to pay, and what to compare between camps.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Camp fees vary widely, so compare what each fee covers and how it can be paid. For the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp, the Kampala intake in December 2026 costs UGX 500,000 or USD 150. The Arua intake in January 2027 costs UGX 400,000 or USD 120. The Summer and Diaspora Camp in July 2027 costs USD 300.

How to pay

A booking fee is paid at registration to hold your son's place. It is UGX 50,000 for Kampala and Arua, and USD 50 for the Summer Camp. It is not refundable and it counts towards the total fee. The balance should be completed seven days before the camp begins. Instalment payments are accepted.

All payments go to our Centenary Bank account, and we do not accept payment to personal phone numbers. If you use MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, send the money to the bank account. A how to pay guide on the website shows the steps for the bank, MTN and Airtel. After paying, upload your receipt on the Attach Proof of Payment page, or add the transaction number, so that your payment is recorded quickly. Some parents register first and pay later, and others pay first and register later. Either way works.

About discounts

We do not offer discounts for now, including for brothers who register together. We want to keep the camp sustainable. We hope to offer them in future as the camp becomes more financially self-sustaining. We encourage parents to register early and to pay in full, or at least pay the non-refundable booking fee.

What to compare between camps

Ask what the fee covers and what costs extra. Ask what the refund policy is. Ask how many mentors work with the boys and what follow up exists after the camp. Public health guides for parents suggest asking about additional costs for optional activities and about the refund policy, so that nothing surprises you later. Our fee is for the seven days of the camp, and our team will gladly explain anything in it that you wish to understand.

The cost in perspective

Many parents spend freely on school fees so that their sons can pass exams. The camp prepares them for what comes after, such as handling money, choosing friends, managing anger and treating others with respect. These are things that formal education and parenting most times miss, and they shape a young man's life for decades.
