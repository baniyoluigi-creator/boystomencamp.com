A Question Parents Ask Us

Is there a mentorship camp for boys in Uganda?

Yes. Boys to Men Mentorship Camp is a week long residential camp in Uganda for boys aged 8 to 23. See dates, venues and how to register.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Yes. Boys to Men Mentorship Camp is a week long residential camp in Uganda for boys and young men aged 8 to 23. I started it because I believe every boy carries potential that deserves to be discovered and developed early.

Boys are grouped by age into Young Explorers (8 to 10), Path Finders (11 to 13), Rising Men (14 to 17) and Young Men (18 to 23). During the week they learn from several of our more than 20 mentors. The sessions deal with identity, a vision and plan for life, money, confidence, managing anger, respect for others, and brotherhood.

The camp is not a military camp and it is not a place of punishment. It is for any boy who is ready to grow, whether he is doing well already or needs a fresh start. Boys have joined us from five countries, so a boy from Kampala, Arua or abroad will find others like him.
