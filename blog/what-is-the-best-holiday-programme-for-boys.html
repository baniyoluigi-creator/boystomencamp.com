A Question We Hear Often

What is the best holiday programme for boys?

Not all holiday programmes are built for the same purpose. Here is what actually makes one worth choosing over another.

Many parents ask us this while comparing several options at once, and it is a fair question, because not all holiday programmes are built for the same purpose.

Some programmes are mostly about fun and keeping boys busy. That has its place, but it does not usually change how a boy sees himself.

Some are mostly academic, catching up on schoolwork. Useful, but it does not touch the emotional and character side of a boy's growth.

“The best holiday programme for a boy is one that does more than keep him busy, structured days, real challenges, honest mentorship, and space to reflect.”

Some are sports camps, which build discipline and teamwork, but rarely go deeper into a boy's inner life, his fears, his identity, his purpose.

The best holiday programme for a boy is one that does more than one of these at the same time, structured days, real challenges, honest mentorship, and time built in for reflection, not just activity.

We designed our own programme with this in mind. Boys are active, yes, but they are also asked hard questions, given real responsibility, and given space to talk about things they usually keep to themselves.

If you are choosing between programmes, ask what a typical day actually includes, not just games and meals, but real conversations and real growth moments.
