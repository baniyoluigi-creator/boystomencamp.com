A Question Parents Ask Us

When and where are the next Boys to Men camps in Uganda?

Boys to Men camps run in Kampala in December, Arua in January and a Summer Camp each July. See the dates, venues and fees.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

We run three camps in the coming season, and each one is open to boys from anywhere in Uganda and from abroad.

The Kampala camp runs from December 13 to 19, 2026 at St. Elizabeth SSS Nkoowe. The fee is UGX 500,000 or USD 150. The Arua camp runs from January 10 to 16, 2027 at St. Joseph's College Ombaci. The fee is UGX 400,000 or USD 120. The Summer and Diaspora Camp runs from July 7 to 16, 2027 in Kampala. The fee is USD 300.

Our most recent camp was in Gulu in May 2026. There is no Gulu camp in the current schedule, so if you would like one nearer to you, call us and tell us.

To hold a place, parents pay a booking fee of UGX 50,000 for Kampala or Arua, or USD 50 for the Summer Camp. It counts towards the full fee. The balance is completed seven days before the camp.

Check our upcoming camps and speak to us before you decide.

  • Kampala, 13 to 19 December 2026
  • Arua, 10 to 16 January 2027
  • Summer and Diaspora Camp, Kampala, 7 to 16 July 2027
