A Question Parents Ask Us

What age can a child join a mentorship camp?

Boys from 8 to 23 years old can join the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp in Uganda, in four age groups. Here is how each group is arranged and why older young men are included.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Boys from 8 to 23 years old can join the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp. Each boy is placed in an age group made for his stage, so a boy of ten is not expected to keep pace with a young man of twenty.

The four age groups

Young Men, from 23 down to 18, are at the threshold of independence. They face university, first jobs, money, relationships and big decisions.

Rising Men, from 17 down to 14, are in the teenage years of identity, friendships, pressure from peers and growing independence from home.

Path Finders, from 13 down to 11, are entering adolescence and meeting the first changes in body, feelings and friendships.

Young Explorers, from 10 down to 8, are curious and quick to learn. Habits and values formed at this age often last.

Why we go up to 23

Most programmes in Uganda stop at 17. Boys to Men keeps going, with a dedicated space for young men aged 18 to 23. This includes Senior 6 vacationists waiting to start university, who can use the time to prepare with more focus, and young men who feel they were never taught how to handle money, relationships or direction. The Summer Camp in July also suits this group.

When is the best time to start

The earlier a boy starts, the more time there is for habits to form. Our curriculum has four levels that a boy can move through over roughly three to four years, and the American Camp Association found that benefits grow when boys return over several seasons. Yet no age is too late to begin, and a young man of 22 can gain a great deal from a week of honest conversation.

For families with brothers

If you have sons in different age groups, they can attend the same camp and are placed in groups suited to them. There are no discounts for brothers for now. Each boy receives his own registration code that begins with BTM, and each boy is called by our team before the camp. Parents are also welcome to join the parents session and the WhatsApp platform, which serve the whole family.

Not sure which group fits

Boys sometimes sit between two groups. Telephone us and tell us about your son. We will help you choose.
