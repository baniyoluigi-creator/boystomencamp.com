A Question We Hear Often

What happens at a boys mentorship camp day to day?

A good day is built from several kinds of moments, not constant activity or constant lecture. Here is a full day, start to finish.

Parents often imagine camp as either constant activity or constant lecture. In reality, a good day is built from several different kinds of moments.

Mornings usually begin with physical activity, waking the body up and building discipline through movement.

Mid mornings move into structured sessions, real conversations and challenges around confidence, responsibility, purpose, and character.

“By the end of a single day, a boy has usually moved through activity, challenge, connection, and stillness.”

Afternoons often include group tasks, boys working together, sometimes failing together, and learning through the experience rather than a lecture about it.

Evenings slow down, space for reflection, honest conversation around the fire, and quieter one on one time with mentors.

By the end of a single day, a boy has usually moved through activity, challenge, connection, and stillness, which is exactly the mix that produces real, lasting change rather than a short lived feeling.
