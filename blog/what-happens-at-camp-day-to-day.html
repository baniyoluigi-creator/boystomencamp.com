How long is a holiday camp and what happens each day?

How long the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp lasts in Kampala, Arua and the July Summer Camp, and what a boy experiences from the first day to the last.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

The Kampala and Arua camps each run for seven days. Kampala meets from 13 to 19 December 2026 and Arua from 10 to 16 January 2027. The Summer and Diaspora Camp in Kampala runs from 7 to 16 July 2027.

What a day contains

Days combine teaching sessions, small group mentorship conversations, physical activities, practical tasks such as cooking and quiet time to reflect. Boys live together, share duties and learn by doing. The sessions use games, role plays, stories, pictures, personal assignments and questions, so a boy is not sitting through lectures all day.

The journey of the week

The week follows three stages that we call Discover, Develop and Deploy.

In the first stage, a boy looks at who he is. This covers identity, purpose, self-awareness and the potential already within him.

In the second stage, he looks at who he can become. This covers personal mastery, practical mastery and people mastery, which are the three corners of what we call the Progress Triangle.

In the third stage, he looks at what he will do with it. He practises leading himself, leading others and creating real impact.

Along the way, boys meet the six Ps of manhood, which are Purpose, Provide, Protect, Precede, Profess and Procreate. They also learn how what a boy is exposed to shapes what he believes, what he does and what results he gets.

Voices from outside

The camp brings in guest speakers. Dr Joel Aita, Chairman of the Council of Muni University, has visited to share the story of the Chinese bamboo tree, which grows unseen for a long time before it rises. Letaru Deborah and Dr Okidi Lawrence have taught sessions on health, nutrition and a responsible lifestyle.

After the last day

The camp does not end when the boys go home. Parents have their own session and a WhatsApp platform, and there is a learning blog. A boy who wishes to continue can move on to the next level in a later camp.

  • Arua, 10 to 16 January 2027
