A Question Parents Ask Us

Can I pay for the camp in instalments?

Yes. Boys to Men parents can pay a booking fee and then pay the balance in instalments before the camp. Here is how it works.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Yes. We know that most families pay from monthly income, so we allow instalments.

The first step is the booking fee, which is UGX 50,000 for the Kampala and Arua camps and USD 50 for the Summer Camp. It holds your son's place and counts towards the total. After that you may pay the rest in instalments, as long as the full balance is complete seven days before the camp.

All payments go into the Centenary Bank account shown on our website. You may send from MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money into that bank account, and the how-to guide on the website shows each step. Please do not send money to any phone number.

After each payment, upload your proof of payment on the website. You will receive a copy, and we issue a receipt for every amount paid, so you always have a record.

The earlier you register, the easier it is to spread the payments.
