A Question Parents Ask Us

Can I pay for the camp with mobile money?

Yes, you can pay Boys to Men camp fees from MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money into our Centenary Bank account. Never pay to a phone number.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Yes, but with one important rule. Camp fees go into our Centenary Bank account and not to any phone number.

You may send from MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money into that bank account. The bank details and a step by step how-to guide are on the Boys to Men website. We have set it up this way so that every payment is recorded properly and no family is cheated.

After you pay, upload your proof of payment on the website, using the screenshot or the transaction ID. You will receive a copy for your records, and we issue a receipt from our Finance Department for every amount paid.

Please be careful. If anyone asks you to pay Boys to Men fees to a personal number, or by any other route, do not pay. Call us first on the numbers below.
