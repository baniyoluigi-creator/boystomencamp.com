A Question We Hear Often

How do I register my son for the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp?

Registration is usually the easiest step. Here is exactly what it involves, from sign up to what comes after camp.

This is often the easiest step in the whole process, but parents still like to know exactly what it involves.

Registration is done directly through our website, where you will find the current intakes, dates, and fees clearly listed.

You will need some basic information about your son, his age, any specific needs he has, and your own contact details so our team can reach you.

“We follow up with everything you need to prepare him, so he arrives ready, not anxious.”

Once registered, our team follows up with everything you need to prepare him, what to pack, what to expect, and how to talk with him beforehand so he arrives ready, not anxious.

After camp, we also share guidance with you on how to support the growth he has started, so it continues at home and does not fade once camp ends.

If you are unsure which intake fits your family, you are welcome to reach out to us directly before registering, and we will help you find the right fit.
