How do I register my son for a holiday camp?

Step by step registration for the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp in Uganda, including the booking fee, proof of payment, the confirmation call and the requirements list.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Registering takes only a few steps. Here they are in order. If you have already done any step, simply ignore it and move to the next.

The steps

  1. Choose the intake and your son's age group. The camps are Kampala from 13 to 19 December 2026, Arua from 10 to 16 January 2027 and the Summer Camp in Kampala from 7 to 16 July 2027.
  2. Open boystomencamp.com, go to the Apply page and fill in the form. If you have more than one son, register each of them.
  3. Pay the booking fee of UGX 50,000, or USD 50 for the Summer Camp. It is not refundable, and it counts towards the total fee. Payment goes to our Centenary Bank account, whose details appear after you submit the form. We do not accept payment to personal phone numbers. If you use MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, send it to the bank account, using the how to pay guide.
  4. If you have not yet done so, upload your receipt on the Attach Proof of Payment page, or add the transaction number. This lets us record your payment quickly. Parents who prefer to pay first can do so and register afterwards.
  5. Expect a confirmation email within 24 hours. Eduardo or Patricia will call you within 48 hours at most. If you have not yet joined the Boys to Men WhatsApp group, you can join it through the invitation in your confirmation.
  6. Within a week of paying the booking fee, the list of things your son should bring will reach you.
  7. Each registered boy receives his own code, which begins with BTM. Our team will also call each boy before the camp to prepare him.
  8. Complete the balance seven days before the camp. Instalments are welcome.

A few helpful notes

Register early, because it gives our team time to speak with you and your son. There are no discounts for now, including for brothers. If you do not hear from us within the times above, please telephone +256 740 132 573 or +256 779 363 354.

You do not need to have everything decided to begin. Registering simply starts the conversation, and you can ask every question you have on the phone.

When you are ready, start with the intake that suits your son.

  • Kampala, 13 to 19 December 2026
  • Arua, 10 to 16 January 2027
  • Summer and Diaspora Camp, Kampala, 7 to 16 July 2027

See all dates and fees or apply here. You may also call +256 740 132 573 or +256 779 363 354.
