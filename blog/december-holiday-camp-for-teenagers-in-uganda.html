Which December holiday camp in Uganda is good for teenagers?

Planning a December holiday camp in Uganda for your teenage son. Dates, how to prepare, how early to register, and why the booking steps matter.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

A good December camp has dates you can plan around, a programme you can explain to your son, and people you can call. Ours runs from 13 to 19 December 2026 in Kampala.

Why December works

School has ended, families are travelling, and boys face long weeks at home. A camp turns some of that time into something that lasts. If December does not suit your family, the Arua camp follows from 10 to 16 January 2027 at St. Joseph's College Ombaci, and the Summer Camp meets in Kampala from 7 to 16 July 2027.

Plan the steps early

Do not leave it to the week before. After you register on our website, a confirmation email follows straight away, and Eduardo or Patricia will call you within 72 hours at most. The booking fee of UGX 50,000 holds your son's place. It is not refundable and it counts towards the full fee. Once it is paid, the list of things your son should bring reaches you within a week. Our team also calls each boy before the camp to prepare him. The balance should be completed seven days before the camp begins, which for the Kampala camp means by 6 December.

Prepare your son

Tell him early, and tell him plainly. Many boys hear the word mentorship and imagine it is a punishment for bad behaviour. Explain that it is a week to discover what he is capable of, to meet other boys and to learn things that school does not teach. Ask what he hopes to gain. Boys who arrive with their own question usually get the most.

A word to the parent who thinks it is too early or too late

We once heard of a parent who said her son was too good to need such a camp. We believe the best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago, and the second best time is now. A boy who is doing well can grow stronger, and a boy who is struggling can find new ground.

Plan your steps from the dates below.

  • Kampala, 13 to 19 December 2026
  • Arua, 10 to 16 January 2027
  • Summer and Diaspora Camp, Kampala, 7 to 16 July 2027
