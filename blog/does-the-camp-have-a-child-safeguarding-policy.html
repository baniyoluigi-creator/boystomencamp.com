A Question Parents Ask Us

Does the camp have a child safeguarding policy?

Yes. Boys to Men Mentorship Camp has a Child Safeguarding Policy that parents can request. Here is why it matters and what to look for.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Yes. Boys to Men has a Child Safeguarding Policy, and we send it to any parent who asks for it. Please ask. You should read it before you decide.

A safeguarding policy is a written promise about how children will be protected and what staff must do if something goes wrong. A good one explains who is responsible, how adults behave around children, how concerns are reported, and what happens next. It should be something staff actually follow, and not a paper kept in a drawer.

When you read any camp's policy, look for plain language, clear names and clear steps. Ask the director to explain it in his own words. If he cannot, the policy is probably for show.

Our approach also depends on openness. Parents are encouraged to call with concerns, boys have adults they can call, and we speak with every family after registration. Nobody should be left wondering.
