What is the refund and withdrawal policy for the camp?

How booking, withdrawals and transfers work at Boys to Men camps, and what to ask any camp before you pay.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

We keep our withdrawal and refund policy clear and in writing, and we share it with every family as soon as the booking fee has been paid. That way you can read it properly and consent to it before anything else is decided.

The booking fee of UGX 50,000 for the Kampala and Arua camps, or USD 50 for the Summer Camp, holds your son's place and counts towards the full camp fee. It is paid into our Centenary Bank account, and you will receive a receipt for it.

The policy sets out what happens if you need to move to a later camp or withdraw, and how timing affects this. It also allows a parent who withdraws a boy to replace him with another boy who fits the camp requirements. Please read it carefully and ask us about anything that is not clear.

Whichever camp you choose, always ask about refunds before paying, and keep a copy of what you are told. If you want the details explained before you register, call us and we will talk it through.
