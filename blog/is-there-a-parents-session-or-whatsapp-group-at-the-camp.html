A Question Parents Ask Us

Is there a parents' session or WhatsApp group at the camp?

Yes. Boys to Men offers a dedicated parents' session, a parents' WhatsApp platform and a learning blog so parents are part of the journey.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Yes. We believe that a boy changes best when his parents are part of the journey, so we give parents their own space.

There is a dedicated parents' session at the camp. There is also a parents' WhatsApp platform where we share updates and where parents ask questions and learn from one another. And there is a learning blog on our website with answers to the questions parents ask us most.

After you register, our team will call you within 72 hours at the latest. The purpose of the call is to answer your questions, and you are welcome to call us sooner if you have any.

We also send each family the camp information and requirements and the guidelines for the boys, so that you and your son both know what to expect.

A boy who goes home to parents who understand what he learned is far more likely to keep growing. That is why we invest in parents as well.
