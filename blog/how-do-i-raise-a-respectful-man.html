How do I raise a respectful man?

What parents can do to raise a boy into a respectful man, including respect for women, elders and himself.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

A respectful man is not born. He is raised, mostly by what he sees. The first lesson is how the men and women around him treat one another.

Show him respect in your home. Let him see how his father treats his mother and how both treat elders, workers and neighbours. A boy who sees respect daily will find it natural. A boy who sees the opposite will copy it.

Teach him that respect is not fear. A boy who obeys only when he is afraid will stop when no one is watching. Explain why, and let him disagree politely.

Teach him to apologise when he is wrong and to say thank you without being reminded. These two habits shape a man's character more than people realise.

Make sure he learns to respect girls and women. We believe this strongly at Boys to Men, and respectful leadership, including towards girls and women, is one of the changes we aim for in each boy.

Finally, let him see other good men. Mentors, teachers and relatives who live what they say can reinforce what you teach at home.
