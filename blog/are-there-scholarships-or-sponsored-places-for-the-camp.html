A Question Parents Ask Us

Are there scholarships or sponsored places for the camp?

Boys to Men has sponsored some boys through the Banio Foundation and welcomes support for full or half scholarships. Ask how to apply.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Yes, we do try to help boys who cannot afford the fee. The Banio Foundation has already sponsored some boys through the camp, and I would like to sponsor many more.

Scholarships can be full or half. They depend on the contributions we are able to raise, so places are limited and cannot be promised in advance. If your son would benefit but the fee is out of reach, call us and tell us your situation honestly. We will tell you what is possible.

If you are in a position to help, you can also sponsor a boy. A single contribution can change one boy's week, and in many cases his direction. Call us to talk about how to give.

We would like to be a camp that no deserving boy is turned away from only because of money. We are not there yet, but we are working towards it.
