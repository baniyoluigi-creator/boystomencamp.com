A Question Parents Ask Us

How is Boys to Men different from school?

Boys to Men covers what formal education and parenting often miss, helping boys prepare for life as adults starting now.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

School teaches subjects. Parents teach values. Yet there is a gap between the two where many boys quietly struggle. Nobody sits down with them to talk about who they are, how to handle money, how to deal with anger or peer pressure, and how to plan a life.

That gap is where we work. Boys to Men is not school, and it is not extra tuition. We cover what formal education and parenting most times miss, so that a boy begins preparing for life as an adult starting now, and not after he has already made costly mistakes.

The style is also different. Boys live together for a week. They learn through stories, discussion, practical work and time with mentors who are not their teachers or parents. Many boys will speak to a mentor about things they would never say at home or in class.

We also hope the camp supports school. One of the changes we aim for is better focus on studies, and parents often tell us they see this in the following term.
