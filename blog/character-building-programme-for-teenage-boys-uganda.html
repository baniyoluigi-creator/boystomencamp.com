A Question Parents Ask Us

Is there a character building programme for teenage boys near me?

Where to find a character building programme for teenage boys in Uganda, how character really grows, and where the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp meets.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

It may be closer than you think. The Boys to Men Mentorship Camp meets in Kampala every December and in Arua every January, and it has also been held in Gulu. Boys travel to join us from across Uganda and from abroad, including the United States and Australia.

How character really grows

We use a simple chain to explain it. What a boy is exposed to, which we call his environment, shapes what he believes. What he believes shapes what he does. What he does produces his results. The environment includes friends, school, home, church or mosque, books, videos, social media and the internet, whether anyone chose it on purpose or not.

So a boy does not become honest because he was told to be honest. He becomes honest when honest people surround him and he gets many chances to practise. Parents carry a heavy part of this. Researchers at the University of Rochester found that teenagers respond far better to warnings from parents who visibly live by their own values.

What to look for in a programme

Look for practice and not only talk. Look for adults whose lives you would be glad for your son to copy. Look for other boys of his age who are being shaped in the same direction. Look for time to reflect, and for follow up after the last day.

What we offer

For seven days the camp changes a boy's environment on purpose. He lives alongside other boys, does real tasks for himself and for others, meets several mentors and works through sessions on identity, purpose, money, anger, respect and brotherhood. When he goes home, his parents have their own session and a WhatsApp platform for continued learning.

If there is no camp near you

Distance should not stop a determined family. The Arua camp is held at St. Joseph's College Ombaci. Choose the one that suits your travel and your son's holiday dates. For older young men who cannot travel, the MEP Masterclass is an online programme, with the next intake starting on 7 February 2027 and running for eight Sundays.
