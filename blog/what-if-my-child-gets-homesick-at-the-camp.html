What if my child gets homesick at the camp?

Homesickness is normal. Here is how parents can prepare a boy, and how Boys to Men supports boys who miss home.

By Banio Luiji Nobert, Founder of Boys to Men Mentorship Camp

Almost every child feels homesick at some point. It is normal, it usually passes within a day or two, and it is often a sign of a loving home.

At Boys to Men, boys keep busy from morning to evening and live alongside other boys, which helps a great deal. Boys are also free to call Eduardo Atibuni or Kokora Patricia whenever they want. If a boy is struggling, an adult who knows him is a phone call away.

You can help before the camp starts. Talk about what the week will be like. Let him pack his own bag. Agree on how you will keep in touch, and tell him that feeling sad on the second night does not mean he must come home. Avoid saying, 'Call me if you are unhappy and I will fetch you', because that promise can make a small feeling much bigger.

If your son has never been away from home, say so when you register. We will keep an eye on him and speak with you.
