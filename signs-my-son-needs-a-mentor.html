A Question We Hear Often

What are the signs my son needs a mentor?

No single sign means something is seriously wrong. But when a few show up together, it is often worth listening to. Here is what to watch for.

Parents often sense something is different before they can name exactly what it is. There are usually a few signs that show up together, not just one.

He has started pulling away from conversations with you, answering in short words where he used to talk freely.

He seems to be figuring out who he is by copying whoever is closest to him, without much guidance from you.

“When a few of these show up together, it is often a sign that your son could use a steady, trustworthy voice outside the home.”

He shows sudden changes in mood or behaviour that do not seem to match anything you can point to directly.

He seems to be carrying something quietly, distracted, distant, or unusually withdrawn, without telling you why.

No single sign on its own means something is seriously wrong. But when a few of these show up together, it is often a sign that your son could use a steady, trustworthy voice outside the home.

Boys to Men Mentorship Camp exists to be exactly that voice, at exactly this stage.
