The Boys to Men Journey

From potential to progress

Every camp, every mentoring conversation, and every follow-up session moves a boy through three stages.

1

Discover

Who he is: identity, purpose, self-awareness, and the potential already within him.

2

Develop

Who he can become: personal mastery, practical mastery, and people mastery.

3

Deploy

What he does with it: leading himself, leading others, and creating real impact.

What Your Son Leaves With

Ten changes parents are really paying for

A camp fee covers days and meals. What parents are truly investing in is a better future for their son. These are the changes we work towards with every boy, from the youngest explorer to the young man waiting to begin university.

1

He knows who he is

His values, purpose, gifts and talents, found and named.

2

He loves school again

More focus, better results, and a lasting commitment to learning.

3

He breaks free

From destructive habits, addictions, bad peer groups and harmful screen use.

4

He heals

Emotional wounds given language at last, and limiting beliefs overcome.

5

He has a plan

A vision for his life, and a plan he owns and works towards after camp.

6

He understands money

Financial literacy, better money habits, and an entrepreneurial mindset and skillset.

7

He stands tall

Confidence without being told who to be, and anger he can manage.

8

He leads with respect

Clear communication, mature leadership, and respect for girls and women.

9

He belongs

A brotherhood of peers and mentors who hold him to his best.

10

He rises for good

Healthy manhood, and growing appreciation for family, community, nation and Africa.

How It Works

Securing a place takes three simple steps

Some parents register first and pay after. Others pay first and register after. Either way works.

1

Choose an intake

Pick Kampala, Arua or the Summer Camp. One intake covers every age from 8 to 23.

2

Register and pay the booking fee

UGX 50,000 (USD 50 for the Summer Camp). It counts toward the fee and is non-refundable. Pay by bank, MTN MoMo or Airtel Money.

3

Receive his confirmation

We confirm his place within 24 hours of receiving the booking fee, and send the preparation checklist.

Parents Ask

Quick answers before you decide

Is the camp safe?

Every mentor is known to the team and supervised throughout camp. Our approach is calm, coaching-based mentorship, not discipline or a bootcamp. Read our safeguarding commitment.

Can boys living in Uganda join the Summer Camp?

Yes. The Summer Camp is open to boys and young men living or studying in Uganda, and to those joining from abroad.

What does it cost, and can we pay in instalments?

Fees are on the Apply page. A non-refundable booking fee secures his place and counts toward the total. Instalment plans are available, with the balance cleared before camp begins.

What if we pay before we register?

That is fine. Register him on the Apply page, choose Yes under the booking fee and add the transaction ID. We will match the payment to his name.
