About the Camp

Prepare Him for the Man He Is Becoming

What if your son became more focused, responsible, disciplined, ambitious and knowledgeable, ready to gladly and ably fulfil what manhood will ask of him?

What do you wish you had learnt when you were younger? What if he could learn those lessons earlier, starting now? Why? Because what we do not learn through mentoring, training, coaching and exposure, we learn through delayed experience, and often through very costly mistakes.

Imagine him knowing who he is, where he is going and the plan to get there. Thinking well, handling money, building the right networks and growing a business. Leading with confidence, speaking with clarity and courage, and collaborating with women. Mastering his anger, healing old emotional wounds and staying free from addiction and bad company. Standing with his brothers and serving his family, community, nation and Africa.

The Boys to Men Mentorship Camp is a seven-day residential programme that prepares boys and young men aged 8 to 23 for life now and in the years ahead. On arrival, each boy joins a cluster for his age, 8 to 10, 11 to 13, 14 to 17 or 18 to 23, so what he learns fits where he is.

Adulthood will not wait for him to be ready. Will you leave his preparation to chance, or join the parents choosing to prepare their sons today?

Intakes

Kampala, Gulu, Arua, and a dedicated Summer Camp

Kampala, Arua, and Gulu each run their own annual intake at different points in the year, with a Summer Camp every July in Kampala. Every intake is open to boys from anywhere, including those living abroad, and the July camp gives families abroad a longer holiday window.

How It Works

Securing a place takes three simple steps

Some parents register first and pay after. Others pay first and register after. Either way works.

1

Choose an intake

Pick Kampala, Arua or the Summer Camp. One intake covers every age from 8 to 23.

2

Register and pay the booking fee

UGX 50,000 (USD 50 for the Summer Camp). It counts toward the fee. It is not refunded at any time after it is paid, even if your son moves to another camp or is withdrawn. Pay by bank, MTN MoMo or Airtel Money.

3

Receive his confirmation

We confirm his place within 24 hours of receiving the booking fee, and send the preparation checklist.

Parents Ask

Quick answers before you decide

Is the camp safe?

Every mentor is known to the team and supervised throughout the camp. Our approach is calm, coaching-based mentorship, not discipline or a bootcamp. Read our safeguarding commitment.

Can boys from abroad join the Kampala, Arua or Gulu camps?

Yes. Every intake is open to boys and young men from anywhere, and past camps have gathered participants from beyond Uganda. The July Summer Camp simply gives families abroad a longer holiday window. It is open to boys in Uganda too, and suits Senior 6 vacationists preparing for university.

What does it cost, and can we pay in instalments?

Fees are on the Apply page. A non-refundable booking fee secures his place and counts toward the total. Instalment plans are available, with the balance cleared before the camp begins.

What if we pay before we register?

That is fine. Register him on the Apply page, choose Yes under the booking fee and add the transaction ID. We will match the payment to his name.
