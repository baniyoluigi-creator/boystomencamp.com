Why You Need This as a Boy or Young Man

This page is for you, not just your parents. You might be reading this because a parent sent you the link, or because you found it yourself. Either way, here is why this matters for you specifically.

For Boys

This is not a punishment, and it is not a school trip

Boys to Men is a space where you get to figure out who you actually are, away from the noise, with men who have been where you are and will not talk down to you.

Whether you are in Kampala, Arua, Gulu, or growing up somewhere far from Uganda, there is a place for you here.

What You Actually Get

Answers to questions you may not have said out loud

Someone who has been there

Mentors who talk about pressure, fear, failure, and identity honestly, not as a lecture, but as people who worked through the same things.

A safe space to ask

Questions about manhood, confidence, money, relationships, and purpose that you may never have felt safe asking anyone else.

A relationship that continues

Camp is the start, not the end. Mentors stay in touch and check in on you well after camp is over.

You carry unexplored potential. Camp exists to help you discover, develop, and deploy it, on your own terms, with men who genuinely want to see you succeed.
