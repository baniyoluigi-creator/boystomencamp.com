Intakes · Summer Camp

Boys to Men Summer Camp

A concentrated, immersive version of the full Boys to Men experience, held in July when families abroad have a longer holiday. Open to everyone, in Uganda and abroad.

8 Days · Kampala Only · Ages 8–23

Next Summer Camp: July 7–16, 2027
Location: Kampala only
Fee: USD 300, covering the full 8-day camp.
Booking fee: USD 50 secures his place. It counts toward the fee. It is not refunded at any time after it is paid, even if your son moves to another camp or is withdrawn.

A UGX equivalent can be arranged on request at the time of payment.

For those travelling from abroad, we help with travel guidance, and mentor follow-up continues after your son returns home. Boys are clustered by age automatically on arrival, so every activity fits where your son actually is.

Who It Is For

Why we run a Summer Camp

Every intake is open to boys and young men from anywhere, including those living abroad. The Summer Camp exists because many families in the diaspora cannot fit the Ugandan school holiday dates, and their summer holiday is longer, so July gives them more time. It suits boys on their summer holidays, including those in international schools and children raised abroad who want to reconnect with the motherland. It is also a good fit for Senior 6 vacationists, preparing them to begin university life with more focus. Boys living in Uganda are welcome too.
