Intakes · Summer Camp

Boys to Men Summer Camp

A concentrated, immersive version of the full Boys to Men experience, open to boys and young men living or studying in Uganda and to those joining from abroad.

8 Days · Kampala Only · Ages 8–23

Next Summer Camp: July 7–16, 2027
Location: Kampala only
Fee: USD 300, covering the full 8-day camp.
Booking fee: USD 50 secures his place. It counts toward the fee and is non-refundable.

A UGX equivalent can be arranged on request at the time of payment.

For those travelling from abroad, we help with travel guidance, and mentor follow-up continues after your son returns home. Boys are clustered by age automatically on arrival, so every activity fits where your son actually is.

Apply for the Summer Camp

Who It Is For

Who the Summer Camp is for

The Summer Camp is for boys and young men on their summer holidays, whether they live in Uganda or abroad. It suits those in international schools, children raised in the diaspora who need to reconnect with the motherland, and young men who are out of school or on vacation while they wait to begin university.

What Your Son Leaves With

Ten changes parents are really paying for

A camp fee covers days and meals. What parents are truly investing in is a better future for their son. These are the changes we work towards with every boy, from the youngest explorer to the young man waiting to begin university.

1

He knows who he is

His values, purpose, gifts and talents, found and named.

2

He loves school again

More focus, better results, and a lasting commitment to learning.

3

He breaks free

From destructive habits, addictions, bad peer groups and harmful screen use.

4

He heals

Emotional wounds given language at last, and limiting beliefs overcome.

5

He has a plan

A vision for his life, and a plan he owns and works towards after camp.

6

He understands money

Financial literacy, better money habits, and an entrepreneurial mindset and skillset.

7

He stands tall

Confidence without being told who to be, and anger he can manage.

8

He leads with respect

Clear communication, mature leadership, and respect for girls and women.

9

He belongs

A brotherhood of peers and mentors who hold him to his best.

10

He rises for good

Healthy manhood, and growing appreciation for family, community, nation and Africa.
