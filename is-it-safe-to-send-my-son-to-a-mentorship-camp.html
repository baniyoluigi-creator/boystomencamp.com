A Question We Hear Often

Is it safe to send my son to a mentorship camp?

This is often the biggest hidden worry behind every other question. Here is what actually keeps a camp safe.

This is often the biggest hidden worry behind every other question a parent asks us, even when it is not said directly.

Safety usually comes down to a few things working together. Who is supervising the boys, how are mentors selected and trained, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Our mentors are carefully selected, not just for experience with boys, but for character and trustworthiness, since they are with your son closely for several days.

“We would rather you ask us every question directly before registering than register with any doubt still sitting in your mind.”

Boys are never left unsupervised in situations that could put them at risk, and there are clear guidelines mentors follow around behaviour, discipline, and communication with parents.

We also stay in contact with families during camp when needed, so you are never left wondering what is happening with your son.

If safety is your biggest concern, and for most parents it is, we would rather you ask us every question directly before registering than register with any doubt still sitting in your mind.
