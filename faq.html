Frequently Asked Questions

How are boys grouped by age?

Boys are placed automatically into age-appropriate clusters, Young Explorers, Path Finders, Rising Men, and Young Men, once they arrive. There is nothing to select in advance.

When do camps run?

Kampala runs every December, Arua's next intake runs 10–16 January 2027, and Gulu's most recent (8th) intake ran in May 2026. The Summer Camp runs every July in Kampala, open to boys in Uganda and abroad. The next Summer Camp is July 7–16, 2027.

How do I pay, and what if I pay before registering?

A booking fee of UGX 50,000 (USD 50 for the Summer Camp) secures his place. It counts toward the fee and is non-refundable. Pay by bank, MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. If you pay first, register him afterwards, choose Yes under the booking fee and add the transaction ID so we can match it to his name. See How to Pay.

Can we pay in instalments?

Yes. Instalment plans are available, and the balance is cleared before camp begins.

Is the camp safe, and who supervises participants?

Every mentor is known to the team and supervised throughout camp. Boys to Men is also not a disciplinary or bootcamp-style programme, our approach is calm, coaching-based mentorship. See our full Safeguarding page.

What happens during camp?

Mentorship conversations, leadership activities, team challenges, reflection sessions, life skills training, and entrepreneurship exposure, structured around our Discover, Develop, Deploy framework.

What should my son bring?

A full preparation checklist is sent after registration is confirmed.

Are the Kampala, Arua and Gulu camps open to boys from the diaspora?

Yes. Every intake is open to boys and young men from anywhere, including those living abroad, and past camps have gathered participants from beyond Uganda.

Why is there a separate Summer Camp?

Many families in the diaspora cannot fit the Ugandan school holiday dates, and their summer holiday is longer. The July Summer Camp gives them more time. It is open to boys in Uganda too, and suits Senior 6 vacationists, preparing them to begin university life with more focus.

Can boys living in Uganda join the Summer Camp, and how does it work from abroad?

Yes. The Summer Camp is open to boys and young men living or studying in Uganda, and to those joining from abroad. If you are travelling, apply for the Summer Camp and the team handling travel guidance, timing and communication across time zones will follow up. See Summer Camp.
