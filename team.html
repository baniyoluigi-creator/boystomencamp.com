Banio Luiji Nobert

Founder and Team Leader, Potential Unexplored Ltd | Lead Facilitator

Banio Luiji Nobert is the Founder and Team Leader of Potential Unexplored Ltd and the vision bearer of the Boys to Men Mentorship Programme. For more than a decade, he has worked with people and organisations at every level, from grassroots communities to corporate boardrooms, with a deep passion for solution focused methods that build individual and institutional capacity.

His journey began with Uganda's Local Government under the NAADS programme, working directly with farmers. He then moved to Caritas Archdiocese of Tororo as Head of Economic Empowerment, where he coordinated the ERI project and trained communities directly in the ERI Approach. He served as a Regional Programme Officer with UNADA in the West Nile region under the DANIDA funded DAR Programme, and then coordinated the ERI East Africa Programme across six partner organisations of horizont3000 for five years. He has also remained active in community level leadership, including his continued leadership of GUWNSAA SACCO, and served as Guild Speaker during his university days. Together, these experiences shaped how he understands people, teams and institutions, and how to lead, train, coach and mentor them toward greater effectiveness.

Today, Banio leads Potential Unexplored Ltd (PUL), a Uganda based company working through two connected arms, Organisational Development and Life Readiness Programmes, with institutions across Uganda and Africa. PUL works from one guiding conviction: self-leadership comes first, and only then can potential be discovered, developed, and deployed in individuals, teams, organisations, and communities. In the past five years alone, Banio and his team have delivered more than 130 training, coaching, and mentorship engagements across humanitarian and refugee response, banking and financial services, agriculture and agribusiness, higher education, youth development, and faith based and community focused organisations. Institutions served include UNHCR, the Bank of Uganda, Centenary Bank, KCB, War Child Canada, CORDAID South Sudan, horizont3000, Opportunity International AgFinance, the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program, and Gulu, Muni, and Makerere Universities.

That experience is still active. PUL is delivering a one year leadership training and coaching programme for more than 30 managers of Opportunity International AgFinance across several countries, alongside a similar engagement for 12 managers of NASHO Computers. On the Life Readiness side, since 2023 PUL has delivered 19 paid public intakes of the MEP Masterclass for adults and 8 intakes of the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp, bringing together hundreds of young men from more than 5 countries, including the USA and Australia.

At the camp, Banio is the Lead Facilitator and the vision bearer behind the programme. He has led the development of a specialised training manual that covers all key sessions at the camp, making it easier to scale the programme across Africa and beyond. Mindset, financial literacy, habits, entrepreneurship, emotional intelligence, goal setting, networking and public speaking are among the topics the boys explore.

He is a GALS Master Trainer and an ERI Approach Master Trainer, and the author of twelve books.

Eduardo Fortunato Atibuni

Managing Director, Boys to Men Mentorship Programme

Eduardo is the Managing Director of the Boys to Men Mentorship Programme, which Potential Unexplored Ltd runs as part of its Life Readiness Programme. He oversees the programme and works closely with the Founder and the rest of the team, so that every intake delivers what families have been promised.

At the camp, Eduardo is one of the facilitators, working alongside the other facilitators, mentors and speakers. He takes the lead on practical skilling for young people and also facilitates sessions on other soft skills. Beyond the camp, he co-facilitates training, coaching and mentoring programmes for organisations and groups in leadership, team building and youth mentorship, and he has built strong competence in each of these areas.

Kokora Patricia

Operations Manager

Patricia is a certified coach with MOGi Global Leadership School, founded by Dr Mike Oladipo. She is also the Operations Manager at Potential Unexplored Ltd, where she supports the coordination and logistics behind the camp. At the camp, she is one of the facilitators, working alongside the other facilitators, mentors and speakers.

Patricia is a firm advocate for the boy child. She believes that mentoring a boy is not a luxury but a necessity for the society he will one day help to lead. For her, it is a way of caring for the boy himself, and it is also an investment in the women he will meet, work alongside, marry and raise children with. A boy who has been guided well learns to listen, to respect and to protect, and those lessons reach his sisters, his wife and his daughters long after the camp has ended.

Patricia holds the family to be sacred, so she never sees a boy on his own. She sees a son, a brother and a future husband and father, and she works to bring parents and guardians into his story. She also joins coaching and mentoring work with organisations and groups in leadership, team building and youth mentorship.
