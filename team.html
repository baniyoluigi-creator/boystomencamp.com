About · Team

The team keeping every intake running

Coordination, communication, and logistics, handled by people who care about getting every detail right for your family.

Banio Luiji Nobert

Founder & Team Leader

Leads Boys to Men Mentorship Camp and Potential Unexplored Ltd, with over a decade of leadership training and coaching experience across Uganda and Africa.

Eduardo Fortunato Atibuni

Managing Director, Boys to Men Mentorship Programme

Eduardo is staff of Potential Unexplored Ltd, which runs the Life Readiness Programmes under which the camps fall. His work is not limited to one region, he goes wherever the programme runs, working closely with the rest of the team to ensure the overall success of the camp and the company's other activities. He is a co-facilitator alongside Nobert (Founder) and the visiting mentors, taking the lead on practical skilling for young people in addition to sessions on other soft skills. Eduardo also co-facilitates training, coaching, and mentoring programmes for organisations and groups in leadership, team building, and youth mentorship activities, and has built strong competence across these areas.
