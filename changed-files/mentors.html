Mentors

The men and women behind the mentorship

Every mentor is known to the team, vetted, and supervised throughout camp. Real business leaders, doctors, engineers, and speakers who show up in person, this is who your son will learn alongside.

Banio Luiji Nobert

Founder and Team Leader, Potential Unexplored Ltd | Lead Facilitator

Banio Luiji Nobert is the vision bearer of the Boys to Men Mentorship Program, an initiative of Potential Unexplored Ltd, where he leads a team of staff working to catalyse the rise of leaders who can effectively lead themselves and lead others.

He believes every great leader begins with one fundamental responsibility: learning to lead oneself, and then eventually leading others. He defines leadership as the ability to demonstrate a vision in words and action that positively affects the thoughts, feelings, and actions of oneself and other people, so that even if you did not require others to, they would still want to follow you.

Banio works from the conviction that leadership has two essential dimensions: self-leadership and people leadership. Self-leadership is the foundation, the ability to understand who we are, what we value, why we exist, and the potential within us. People who lead themselves well become grounded, intentional, and responsible, taking ownership of their thoughts, choices, habits, relationships, finances, and character.

People leadership is the next level: learning how to lead others, whether in a family, a team, an organisation, or a community. Great people leaders do not simply manage tasks. They build trust, create healthy relationships, encourage open communication, handle conflict constructively, develop commitment, and strengthen accountability.

As a corporate trainer and speaker, Nobert has made a name for Potential Unexplored across two fronts: organisational strengthening through leadership development, team building, and sales training, and life readiness programmes for young people and adults across Uganda and Africa.

Over more than a decade, Banio has trained, coached, and mentored individuals and teams across Uganda and Africa, with a client portfolio of more than fifty institutions including UNHCR, Bank of Uganda, Centenary Bank, War Child Canada, CORDAID South Sudan, Mastercard Foundation Scholars programme, horizont3000, and Opportunity International. He coordinated the Enabling Rural Innovation Programme across East Africa for five years and holds GALS Master Trainer and ERI Approach Global Quality Controller certifications. He is the author of twelve books, including The Team Accountability Compass and Heal to Lead.

He has led the development of a specialized training manual that covers all key sessions within the camp, making it easier for the scaling of the program across Africa and beyond.

Alionzi Lawrence Dangote

Mayor, Arua City 2026–2031 | 88th Guild President, Makerere University | Electrical Engineer

The youngest City Mayor in Uganda leading Arua City in West Nile, Lawrence's story is one of the most striking any boy at camp usually hears. Born and raised in West Nile, Dangote rose to become the 88th Guild President of Makerere University, Uganda's oldest and most prestigious institution of higher learning, where he pursued engineering. Upon graduation, he served as Managing Director of Joadah Design Institute.

To have reached elected office at the age of 26 years, in a role most people spend decades working toward, makes Lawrence a living example of what is possible when leadership is taken seriously early. He has spoken to boys at camp about public speaking and leadership, drawing directly on his own path from student leader to engineer to the youngest mayor in Uganda, and pushing every boy in the room to see city hall, not as something distant, but as somewhere he could genuinely stand one day.

Dr. Joel Jaffer A'ita

CEO, Joadah Consult | Chairman, Muni University Council | Pan-African Infrastructure Strategist

Joel is a pan-African infrastructure strategist, consultant, and entrepreneur with a proven record of transforming national development visions into bankable, fundable, and buildable projects. He has led the planning, design, and delivery of large-scale public infrastructure across Africa, spanning tertiary hospitals, universities, water and sanitation systems, roads, and investment-ready urban developments, financed by institutions such as the World Bank, the African Development Bank, the Islamic Development Bank, and the Saudi Fund.

As CEO of Joadah Consult and Chairman of the Muni University Council, Joel operates at the intersection of infrastructure, education, policy, and technology. His work is driven by a belief that infrastructure is not just about buildings and roads but about systems, skills, institutions, and digital platforms that unlock long-term economic value.

Known for combining technical depth with strategic clarity, Joel is widely regarded for his ability to structure complex projects, mobilise financing, and align diverse stakeholders, from governments and development finance institutions to private investors and communities.

Dr. A'ita first addressed the boys at the first ever edition of camp in Arua, speaking on becoming the kind of men Africa urgently needs, and returned as Chief Guest to close the Kampala intake Coronation event. His willingness to return to camp a second time, as its most senior guest, tells every parent that this programme attracts leaders operating at the highest levels of regional industry, and that their sons are being spoken to as future leaders in their own right.

Omia Razak Iganaci

Founder and CEO, Omia Agribusiness Development Group Ltd

An agribusiness leader passionate about transforming smallholder farming in Africa, Razak is the Founder and CEO of Omia Agribusiness Development Group Ltd, working to ensure last-mile access to climate-smart agricultural inputs, extension services, irrigation systems, and market linkages, empowering farmers, refugees, youth, and women.

Growing up in a farming household, he witnessed firsthand the challenges and resilience of rural communities. His passion for agribusiness is deeply personal: his parents were exiled in South Sudan (1980–1990) due to conflict in Uganda, and in 1998, his father was murdered by rebels in Yumbe. These experiences have shaped his commitment to resilience, economic empowerment, and food security.

With over six years of experience leading agribusiness solutions, he has mobilized funding, built strategic partnerships, and integrated digital tools to scale agricultural services. Omia Agribusiness employs 81 full-time staff, all young people, working with partners including Mercy Corps, the World Food Programme, USAID, the Danish Refugee Council, Palladium, Ayuda en Accion, USADF, and SNV.

He leads the West Nile Agribusiness and Renewable Energy Expo, an annual event bringing together 75+ exhibitors and 3,000+ farmers, and is a 2024 Acumen East Africa Fellow, a 2019 Mandela Washington Fellow, and a 2022 One Young World Ambassador.

Daniel Choudry

Founder and Lead Consultant, Daniel Choudry Sales Institute

Daniel is widely regarded as one of Africa's foremost sales training consultants, a reputation earned over more than a decade of work across ten countries on the continent and beyond. He has consulted for over ninety percent of Uganda's banks and insurance companies, holds engagements with the World Bank, and has partnered with prominent regional figures, including Vusi Thembekwayo of South Africa, to host sold-out training events in Kampala.

He continues to run active training and coaching programmes across West Africa and Southern Africa as a sales guru and mindset change specialist dedicated to helping individuals and organisations unlock their full potential. At the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp, Daniel did not simply teach sales technique. He challenged every boy in the room to find their own "Kampala," the driving purpose that once pulled him, as a former child soldier out of Northern Uganda, into national influence, and to become genuinely invested in something worth giving their full effort to.

Daniel Dratibi Drileba

Executive Director, Voice of Restoration International Limited | Mental Health Advocate | Author | Global Speaker

Daniel is one of Uganda's most recognised voices on mental health, an award-winning advocate who has appeared repeatedly on national television and radio to speak openly about trauma, addiction, and healing. Institutions across the country consult him directly on these issues, trusting him to guide staff and communities through struggles that are rarely discussed in public.

As Executive Director of Voice of Restoration International Limited, Daniel brings together a rare combination of credentials: published author, global speaker, trained counselor, and Certified Financial Literacy Master Trainer, and he is an active member of the Uganda Financial Literacy Association.

At the camp, he takes on some of the hardest conversations boys will have all week, walking them through addiction and substance abuse, empowering them to live free and fulfilling lives.

Thomas Malunda

Senior Regional Manager, Farmer Support Services, Opportunity International | Founder, MT Agro Eco

Thomas is an agriculturalist by profession, passionate about creating a positive impact in the world through research, writing, speaking, and innovation. He carries vast experience working with Ag-tech start-ups, agribusinesses, and farmers across Africa.

Thomas holds a second-class upper Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Land Use and Management from Makerere University, a Master of Science in Agroecology from Uganda Martyrs University Nkozi, and multiple further certifications spanning organic agriculture, sustainable agricultural finance, and digital learning.

He has previously worked with Buikwe District Local Government, the start-ups Wefarm Limited and Inputi Inc, and the Climate Change Institute Uganda NGO. He currently serves Opportunity International as Senior Regional Manager for the Farmer Support Services Team, leading work across the whole of Africa.

Away from work, Thomas practises and preaches agroecology through the MT Agro Eco Farm and the MT Agro Eco Tales YouTube channel, and hosts the Tom Thinking Series. He shares with the boys about life, goals, and emotional intelligence and meditation, a session that empowers boys to process their emotions, gain clarity on the future they want, and delay gratification.

Vincent Matsiko

Founder and Managing Director, Nugsoft Technologies

Vincent's career is a study in what consistency can build over time. He began in 2014 as head of the ICT department at Biguli SS, before moving through roles as a software developer at Waoni Technologies, a customer care advisor at Uganda Telecom, and a team leader and software developer at Landsat ICT Solutions.

From 2017 to 2018 he led development of the Tuberculosis Laboratory Information System for the Institute of Human Virology Nigeria, deploying it across eight government TB labs, and went on to lead Uganda's first Freshwater Biodiversity Data Portal and a joint Ugandan and Malawian biodiversity data portal.

Today he leads Nugsoft Technologies, the company behind widely used systems including School Monitor, ClinicPlus, Mfuko, PosCream, and PoSCafe, along with a platform now used by over six hundred churches under Namirembe Diocese. Employing over thirty staff, Vincent shows boys that a genuine tech empire can be built from nothing but curiosity and follow-through. He speaks to boys about using technology to build businesses and attain financial progress, not negative addiction.

Benard Alamiga Oku

Founder and Managing Director, Wonderland Farm Services SMC Limited

Benard leads Wonderland Farm Services, an agricultural enterprise whose operations have grown into a genuine beacon of innovation, expanding across West Nile and into the Democratic Republic of Congo. The company currently hosts over thirty interns, giving young people hands-on experience in modern agricultural practice.

His leadership reaches well beyond his own company. Benard chairs the West Nile Agro Input Dealers' Association, sits on the board of the Gulu University West Nile Students and Alumnus Association SACCO, and serves on both the Koboko District Investment Committee and the Koboko District Agriculture Sector Committee as Treasurer.

At camp, Benard speaks directly to what many boys assume is impossible: building something real without significant starting capital. His session lays out practical business ideas boys can start with what they already have, grounded in his own journey from instructor to founder of a company now operating across two countries.

Nathan Odeke

Founder, NASHO Computers

Nathan started the great NASHO Computers as an idea while still at university, with no family wealth, no major connections, and no guarantee it would work. Today it is a genuine multi-branch enterprise, importing and distributing technology products, household appliances, and solar energy solutions across Uganda and neighboring countries.

What makes Nathan such a valuable presence at camp is his honesty about the road it took to get there. He speaks openly about the hard and uncertain early days, the failures that came before any real progress, and the questions most young men are too embarrassed to ask out loud: how do you start a business while still in school, and how do you keep going when nothing seems to be working. He is very open about his early behaviour dysfunctions, showing up not as a perfect person, but as one who lost his way and now understands the power of the environment, friends, and decisions we make. Passionate about God, he inspires many to live purposefully.

Nathan has now spoken to two consecutive camp cohorts. His story, of a young man who built something real from almost nothing, gives every boy in the room permission to believe the same is possible for him.

Dr. Lawrence Okidi (PhD)

Co-founder and Director of Research, Capacity Beyond Ltd | Lecturer, Gulu University

Lawrence is a co-founder of Capacity Beyond Ltd, where he also serves as Director of Research. With over a decade of experience spanning academia and community work across East Africa, he brings a depth of research grounding to the camp's mentorship work that few facilitators can match.

As a lecturer in Gulu University's Faculty of Agriculture and Environment, Lawrence has spent years inspiring students to reach their full potential, and that same patient, invested approach carries directly into his time with the boys. His academic achievements alone give the boys something to aim for, but it is his willingness to sit down one-on-one, offering personal guidance to boys who most need someone to talk to, that leaves the deepest impression.

Next mentor: Gala Winfred → Back to all mentors

Gala Winfred

Confidence Coach, Speaker, Author, and Social Development Practitioner

Gala Winfred is a confidence coach, speaker, author, and seasoned social development practitioner with over eight years of experience implementing humanitarian and development programmes, spanning child protection, education, leadership development, entrepreneurship, vocational skilling, health, safety, and livelihoods.

Inspired by her professional and personal experiences, she founded Gala Legacy Company Limited (GLCL), a Christ-centered organization dedicated to self-mastery, personal development, and emotional restoration, helping individuals rediscover their identity and unlock their God-given potential.

A passionate advocate for personal growth, Gala is also a feminist and a champion of positive male engagement, believing that empowering women and developing responsible, emotionally healthy men are complementary goals that create stronger families and communities.

As the Global Facilitator for the MOGI Global Leadership School and a mentor with Bible Christian Mission (BCM), Gala has trained diverse audiences across countries on identity, self-worth, leadership, and purposeful living. She has also served with Yukon Software SMC, SIHA Network Uganda, FIDA Uganda, Compassion International, Scripture Union, CECI, and Voice of Restoration.

At the Boys to Men Mentorship Camp, Gala brings a unique and invaluable perspective as one of the few female mentors, challenging boys to understand themselves before seeking to lead others, while helping them appreciate, respect, and effectively collaborate with empowered women. Her sessions on confidence, identity, emotional wellbeing, and healthy masculinity encourage participants to become men of character, purpose, and influence.
