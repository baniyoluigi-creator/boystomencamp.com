KEEP "BOYS TO MEN MASTER REGISTRATIONS" UP TO DATE EVERY 5 MINUTES

This adds new boys to the master sheet by itself, under the right camp, newest first.
It never deletes a row and never changes anything you typed. It only adds new boys,
numbers the rows again, fixes the Subtotal and TOTAL rows, and writes each boy's code
(BTM-2650 and so on) in a new column Q, so every boy is recognised for good.
Your own test registrations are left out.


STEP 1. Add the code

1. Open the sheet "Boys to Men Registrations", then Extensions, then Apps Script.
2. Scroll to the very bottom of the script.
3. Open SYNC-MASTER-REGISTRATIONS.gs from this folder, copy ALL of it, and paste it
   after the last line. Do not delete anything that is already there.
4. Click the save icon.


STEP 2. Look first (changes nothing)

1. In the function list at the top, choose previewMasterSync, then click Run.
2. Click Execution log. It tells you how many boys it found, and which ones it would add.
3. If the list looks right, go on. If a boy is missing or in the wrong camp, tell Claude.


STEP 3. Start it

1. Choose startMasterSync, then click Run.
2. It first saves a backup copy of the master sheet in your Drive, then starts the
   5 minute timer, then does the first update.
3. Google may ask you to authorise again. Click Authorise access, choose your account,
   then Advanced, then "Go to (project name) (unsafe)", then Allow.

To stop it at any time, choose stopMasterSync and click Run.


GOOD TO KNOW

* It only looks at the Registrations tab, and it skips quickly when nothing is new.
* A boy is matched to an existing row by his code, or by his name and email. Rows you
  typed by hand stay as they are. The first time a boy is matched, his code is written in column Q.
* New rows start with Reply status "New". Fee and Booking fee due are filled for
  Kampala (UGX 500,000 and 50,000) and Arua (UGX 400,000 and 50,000). The Summer Camp
  is paid in dollars, so those two cells are left empty for you.
* Paid so far, Added to WhatsApp and Notes are for you to fill, as before.
* If a boy shows "Possible duplicate" in Registrations, the Notes cell says so.
* A boy whose camp cannot be read is not added. The preview lists him.
* If you ever run setupTriggers and it clears all timers, run startMasterSync again.
