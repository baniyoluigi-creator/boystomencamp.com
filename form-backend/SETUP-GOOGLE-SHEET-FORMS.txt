SETUP: SENDING WEBSITE FORMS TO A GOOGLE SHEET
Boys to Men Mentorship Camp (boystomencamp.com)

This replaces Formspree. There is no monthly limit. Every registration and
contact message is saved in a Google Sheet, and you also get an email.
It takes about 10 minutes, once.


PART 1: Create the sheet and the script

1. Sign in to Google with baniyoluigi@gmail.com and open sheets.new
   (this creates a new blank Google Sheet).
2. Name the sheet "Boys to Men Registrations".
3. In the menu, click Extensions, then Apps Script.
4. Delete everything in the editor.
5. Open the file google-apps-script.gs from this folder, copy ALL of it,
   and paste it into the editor.
6. Click the save icon (or press Ctrl+S).


PART 2: Publish it

1. Click Deploy (top right), then New deployment.
2. Click the small gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
3. Set these three things:
      Description:      Boys to Men forms
      Execute as:       Me
      Who has access:   Anyone
4. Click Deploy.
5. Google will ask you to authorise. Click Authorise access, choose your
   account, then Advanced, then "Go to (project name) (unsafe)", then Allow.
   This is normal for your own script. It only needs to write to this sheet
   and send you email.
6. Copy the Web app URL. It starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/
   and ends with /exec


PART 3: Connect it to the website

1. Open main.js in your GitHub repository (github.com/baniyoluigi-creator/
   boystomencamp.com) and click the pencil icon to edit.
2. Near the bottom, find this line:
      var SHEET_ENDPOINT = '';
3. Paste your Web app URL between the two quotes, like this:
      var SHEET_ENDPOINT = 'https://script.google.com/macros/s/XXXXXXXX/exec';
4. Click Commit changes.

Also upload the updated apply.html and contact.html from this folder
(Add file, then Upload files, then Commit changes). They carry two small
additions that tell the sheet which form the entry came from.

If you do Part 3 before Part 2 is finished, nothing breaks: while the line
is empty, the forms keep using Formspree.


PART 4: Test it

1. Wait about a minute for GitHub Pages to update.
2. Open boystomencamp.com/contact.html in a fresh browser tab and send a
   test message. Wait at least 8 seconds before pressing Submit on the
   registration form, because the spam guard needs that time.
3. Check the sheet. A tab called "Contact Messages" appears with your
   test entry, and an email arrives in your inbox.
4. Repeat with the registration form. Its entries go to a tab called
   "Registrations".
5. Delete the test rows afterwards.


GOOD TO KNOW

* If you ever edit the script, use Deploy, then Manage deployments, then the
  pencil icon, then Version: New version, then Deploy. The link stays the same.
* Free Gmail can send about 100 notification emails a day. If that is ever
  reached, entries are still saved in the sheet; only the email is skipped.
* To send notices to more people, put their addresses in NOTIFY_EMAIL at
  the top of the script, separated by commas.
* New form fields appear in the sheet as new columns automatically.
* Share the sheet with your team (Share button) so they can follow up.
* Once everything works you can close or delete the Formspree form.
