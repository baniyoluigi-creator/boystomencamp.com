CHECK EVERY PAYMENT AGAINST THE BANK AND SEND THE RECEIPT BY ITSELF

This watches the proof of payment tab. Every minute it looks at payments that are at least
3 minutes old, finds the matching Centenary alert in your Gmail, and when everything agrees
it makes the Finance Department receipt and emails it to the parent with a copy of their proof.
Nothing is ever deleted, and the live script is not changed.

What "everything agrees" means
The bank reference the parent typed, the amount and the date all match one alert. If a parent
gave no reference, the amount, the date and the depositor's name must match one alert only.
If nothing matches yet, it tries again after 10 minutes, 30 minutes and 2 hours. After that
the row is marked Needs Review and you get one email.
Each bank reference can be used once only, so one payment can never confirm two families.


STEP 1. Add the code

1. Sign in with baniyoluigi@gmail.com, open the sheet "Boys to Men Registrations", then
   Extensions, then Apps Script.
2. Scroll to the very bottom of the script.
3. Open VERIFY-PAYMENTS.gs from this folder, copy ALL of it, and paste it after the last
   line. Do not delete anything that is already there.
4. Near the top of what you pasted, check RECEIPT_ADDRESS. It is already set to the address
   from the approved receipt (Jane Frances Plaza, Room 22, 2nd Floor, Wakiso Town, Uganda,
   P.O. Box 210680, Kampala GPO). Change it only if it is wrong.
5. Click the save icon.


STEP 2. Look first (changes nothing)

1. In the function list at the top, choose vpPreview, then click Run.
2. Google will ask you to authorise. Click Authorise access, choose your account, then
   Advanced, then "Go to (project name) (unsafe)", then Allow. It needs Gmail to read the
   bank alerts and send receipts, and Drive to save the receipts.
3. Click Execution log. It says what it would do with each payment, and sends nothing.
4. If a payment you know is genuine shows Needs Review, tell Claude what the log says.


STEP 3. Start it

1. Choose vpStart, then click Run.
2. It starts the one minute timer and remembers this moment as the start. Payments uploaded
   before this moment are left alone, so nobody gets a surprise receipt for an old payment.
   To include older payments too, write a date in INCLUDE_ROWS_FROM, for example
   '2026-11-01 00:00', before you run vpStart.

To stop it at any time, choose vpStop and click Run. It stops only this check.


WHAT YOU WILL SEE IN THE PROOF TAB

New columns appear on the right. Verify Status is Pending, Verified or Needs Review. Verify
Note says why. Bank Ref, Bank Amount and Bank Time show the alert that was matched. Receipt No,
Receipt Link and Receipt Sent At show the receipt. A new tab called Bank Refs Used keeps every
bank reference that has been matched. Receipts are saved in Finance Receipts inside Potential
Unexplored Database in your Drive.

To confirm a payment yourself, type Approved in its Verify Status cell. If you have the bank
reference, type it in Bank Ref. Within a minute the receipt goes out.


GOOD TO KNOW

* Payments in dollars always go to Needs Review, because the bank alerts show shillings only.
* A parent with no email address on the row is looked up by phone number in the Registrations
  tab. If none is found the receipt is saved in Drive and you are told.
* The receipt is the cream Finance Department design approved on 6 November, with both logos,
  the boy codes, the camp fee for the number of boys, the total paid to date, the balance and
  its due date (or "paid in full"), and the list of earlier payments from the same phone.
* Receipt numbers run BTM-RCT-000091, BTM-RCT-000092 and so on. Numbers 87 to 90 were made by
  hand. To start from another number, change FIRST_RECEIPT_NO before the first run.
* The balance counts only payments this system has verified. If the same phone has earlier
  proof rows that were not verified here, you get an email asking you to check that balance.
* CAMP DOCUMENTS. SEND_CAMP_DOCUMENTS is false. Leave it false if the live script already
  sends the documents. If you set it to true, the documents go out straight after the proof
  is uploaded, and the guidelines are NEVER sent without the admission letter. Each boy's letter
  goes with the guidelines every time, and the generic Camp Guidelines file is never sent alone. If any boy has no letter in Camp Documents yet, nothing is sent,
  the row says so, and you get an email. Save the letter, then clear that cell to send.
  Letters are found by the name "Admission Letter and Guidelines- [Boy's Full Name]- Boys to Men"
  or "Admission Letter- [Boy's Full Name]- Boys to Men". The combined file already holds the
  guidelines. A plain letter is sent together with the Camp Guidelines file.
* A free Gmail account can send about 100 emails a day. Each receipt is one email.
