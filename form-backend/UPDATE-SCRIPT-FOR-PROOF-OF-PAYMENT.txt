UPDATE: LET PARENTS ATTACH PROOF OF PAYMENT ON THE REGISTRATION FORM
Boys to Men Mentorship Camp (boystomencamp.com)

The website already asks parents for a transaction ID. This update also lets
them attach a screenshot or PDF. Pictures are saved in a private folder in
your Google Drive called "Boys to Men Payment Proofs", and a link to each
one appears in a new column called "Proof of Payment Link" in the sheet.

Do this once. It keeps the same web app link, so nothing on the website
changes until the last step.


PART 1: Replace the script

1. Sign in to Google with baniyoluigi@gmail.com and open the sheet
   "Boys to Men Registrations".
2. Click Extensions, then Apps Script.
3. Delete everything in the editor.
4. Open google-apps-script.gs from this folder (the new copy), copy ALL of
   it, and paste it into the editor.
5. Click the save icon.


PART 2: Publish the new version (same link)

1. Click Deploy, then Manage deployments.
2. Click the pencil (edit) icon on your existing deployment.
3. Under Version, choose "New version".
4. Click Deploy.
5. Google will ask for one more permission, to use Google Drive. Click
   Authorise access, choose your account, then Advanced, then
   "Go to (project name) (unsafe)", then Allow. This is normal. It only
   needs Drive to save the payment pictures.

Please do not click "New deployment". That would create a new link.


PART 3: Switch it on in the website

1. Tell Claude that the script is updated, and it will switch the upload on.
   Or open main.js on GitHub and change this line:
      var PROOF_UPLOAD_ENABLED = false;
   to:
      var PROOF_UPLOAD_ENABLED = true;
2. Wait about a minute, then test: open the Apply page in a fresh tab, fill
   the form, choose "Yes (I have paid)", attach a screenshot, and submit.
   Check that a new row appears with a link in "Proof of Payment Link".

IMPORTANT: do not switch the upload on before Part 2 is finished. The old
script cannot handle the picture data and the registration could fail.
