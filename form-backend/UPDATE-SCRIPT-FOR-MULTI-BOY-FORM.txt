UPDATE: SAVE SEVERAL BOYS FROM ONE REGISTRATION
Boys to Men Mentorship Camp (boystomencamp.com)

The new registration form lets a parent or helper register several boys in
one go. This update teaches your Google script to save it properly:

- One row in the "Registrations" sheet for each boy.
- Boys registered together share a Family ID, so you can see who belongs
  together.
- The payment amount, transaction ID and proof are written on the first boy's
  row only, so a family's payment is never counted twice.
- You get ONE alert email per family, with a "TO DO" list at the bottom for
  parents who need adding to the WhatsApp group.
- New columns appear at the end of the sheet by themselves. Your old columns
  and old rows are not touched.

Do this once. It keeps the same web app link.


PART 1: Replace the script

1. Sign in to Google with baniyoluigi@gmail.com and open the sheet
   "Boys to Men Registrations".
2. Click Extensions, then Apps Script.
3. Delete everything in the editor.
4. Open google-apps-script.gs from this folder (the new copy), copy ALL of
   it, and paste it into the editor.
5. Click the save icon.


PART 2: Publish the new version (same link)

1. Click Deploy, then Manage deployments.
2. Click the pencil (edit) icon on your existing deployment.
3. Under Version, choose "New version".
4. Click Deploy.

Please do not click "New deployment". That would create a new link.


PART 3: Tell Claude

Say "script updated". The new form then goes live on the Apply page.
Until then, the current form keeps working as it is.
