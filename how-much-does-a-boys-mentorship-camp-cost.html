A Question We Hear Often

How much does a boys mentorship camp cost?

A fair, honest answer, not vague language. Here is what actually goes into the cost, and what our fee covers.

This is a fair and honest question, and parents deserve a clear answer, not vague language.

Cost usually depends on a few things, the length of the camp, the location, and what is included, meals, accommodation, materials, and mentorship time.

Some parents worry that a lower price means lower quality, and some worry that a higher price means it is out of reach. Neither is always true. What matters most is what is actually happening during those days, not just the number on the invoice.

“What matters most is what is actually happening during those days, not just the number on the invoice.”

At our camps, the fee covers full accommodation, meals, all activities, and full access to our mentors throughout the programme, so there are no hidden costs added later.

We also understand that for many families, this is a real financial decision, not a small one, so we try to be as clear as possible about what is included from the start.
